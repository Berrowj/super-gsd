05 / Evidence coming back here

Daily copies you can verify.
Not “the transfer probably worked”.

DEVCP retains evidence while this PC is offline. Windows pulls the retained bundles when it is awake, signed in, connected and able to authenticate through SSH.

01 · EVERY MINUTECheck collection health

DEVCP runs bounded, lightweight checks through its user cron schedule. Incidents persist even with the browser closed.

02 · DAILY, AFTER 06:00 UTCAudit and package

Run the existing integrity audit. Snapshot sanitized evidence with per-file sizes, times and SHA-256 hashes.

03 · PC CHECKS EVERY 5 MINUTESPull what is missing

Fetch incident updates and new daily bundles. Missed runs catch up at logon or when the schedule resumes.

04 · BEFORE MARKING SUCCESSVerify locally

Check every manifest entry, size and hash. Publish the dated copy only after verification; record the receipt.

Two checks, two different answers

“Hash verified” means the copied files match the captured bundle.

“Capture healthy” is a separate judgement about coverage, freshness and integrity. A verified copy can contain a WARN or FAIL audit—and must keep that evidence.

Privacy, live-file handling and storage safeguards

Only the evidence we need

No prompts, transcripts, raw CLI spools, credentials or whole-project archives. Paths are allowlisted; unsafe files are rejected. Live append-only files are copied as complete-line prefixes with recorded lengths, not described as one atomic fleet-wide snapshot.

No silent deletion

Proposed export budgets: 10 GiB on DEVCP, 20 GiB on this PC. Warn at 80%; stop new copies/bundles at the limit and retain prior evidence. These are separate from Atlas’s existing capture limits.

06 / A useful week of evidence

Review usage and waste.
With the missing parts visible.

The target is a defensible weekly review, not a promise of complete billing data or a rewrite of SGSD’s execution engine.

What the evidence should let us assess

  • Which Clarity projects, worktrees and sessions were covered—and when they were not.
  • Observed token usage by the identities and roles the records actually contain, without counting cached/reasoning subsets twice.
  • Which gate, MUDA and ATC outcomes were observed, and what missing or rejected evidence limits our conclusions.
  • Whether collection or copying stopped during the trial, with preserved incident and recovery history.

What “finished” must prove

  1. A real Atlas panel accessible from this PC.
  2. Automatic checks with execution evidence, not just installed jobs.
  3. One real off-host baseline, rehashed successfully here.
  4. Failure/recovery and missed-copy behaviour tested safely.

Old sessions remain visible gaps until safely refreshed. No synthetic production records or fresh model calls are needed for this feature’s tests.

Next step: review this design, then implement under a Phase 170 plan and deploy through the normal SGSD update after the applicable checks. Windows collection parity, Researcher model selection and formal milestone closure remain separate work.
