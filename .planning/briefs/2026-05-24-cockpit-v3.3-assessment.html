The cockpit explains itself.

v3.3 translates the v3.1 chronicle's answer-first explanation grammar into a live cockpit. The operator gets full contextual awareness during the run — not just at phase close. This page is the explainer of that brief, applying its own 17 design principles to itself so you can react to the grammar visually.

Step 3 · Reflective rationale (on demand)

Why this phase matters — the rationale layer.

Norman's third level (reflective). Heath's SUCCES test for stickiness. Munroe's ten-hundred constraint for plain language. Duarte's what-is / what-could-be beat for the story arc. The operator never has to scan logs to find this — it's curated, always one click away.

Band 3 · Rationale / Story (reflective · drill-in)
Context v3.2 closed the answer-first cockpit-sidecar (compact, 405 lines, 18/18 self-test). The PowerShell live monitor (2,446 lines, 15 panels) is the surface the operator actually watches, and it pre-dates the v3.2 answer-first discipline. Two complaints (freshness + usefulness) drove this brief.

Step 5 · Citations & principles

17 cited design principles, with provenance.

Each principle maps to a specific cockpit decision. Citation chips show provenance: VTP-SUBSTRATE = indexed and queried; CANONICAL = book in operator folder, not yet indexed, applied from training knowledge; OPERATOR = operator's own validated pattern.

P1Sharp-edged 100% stages

Every stage is binary: ✓ done / ⏳ active / ⏸ pending / 🛑 blocked. No percent fields. Wires to existing semantic_acceptance_criteria schema (P97.5 / DLB-07).

Step 6 · Visual frames

Roam 6×6 — every data type gets its native shape.

Dan Roam's argument: there are six kinds of questions humans ask, and six matching visual frames. Use the wrong frame and the picture lies. Use the right one and the answer jumps off the page.

WHO / WHAT
→ portrait
Band 1 supporting + Band 2 stage-owner cells. The active phase, the current agent.
📊
HOW MUCH
→ chart / sparkline
Band 2 trend strip (fog · dispatches · tokens). Inline 12-px sparklines.
⏱
WHEN
→ timeline / pipeline
Band 2 STAGE PIPELINE. The six named stages strip with progress + SLA.
🗺
WHERE
→ dependency map
Band 3 UNLOCKS / WHY anchor. Small graph render of phase ↔ neighbour.
↦
HOW
→ flowchart
Band 2 WHY-RUNNING when extension fired. Gate-chain recovery path.
💭
WHY
→ multi-variable text
Band 3 WHY THIS PHASE. The rationale block — concrete, cited, story-shaped.

Why P132 was inserted (2026-05-24 architectural amendment)

The original 6-phase brief ended with monitor migration. After discussing primary-surface choice, the localhost HTML cockpit became the obvious lead: shares grammar with chronicle + sidecar --html (one design system, one conformance gate covers all four surfaces), avoids 100-line-of-PowerShell-per-panel extension cost, and v3.2's --html mode already does 60% of the work. Lag is not a concern at SGSD's grain — 1-2s SSE freshness is well below stage-transition rate (5s-30min). PowerShell monitor stays as terminal fallback during transition; P133 scope shrinks correspondingly.
